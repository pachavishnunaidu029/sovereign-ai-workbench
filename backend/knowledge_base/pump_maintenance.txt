PUMP MAINTENANCE PROCEDURE

Document ID: SOP-P204-001

Equipment: Cooling Water Pump P-204

1. PURPOSE

This procedure defines the maintenance requirements for Cooling Water Pump P-204.

2. MECHANICAL SEAL

The mechanical seal must be inspected for leakage during routine maintenance.

Minor leakage should be monitored and recorded.

If leakage increases or becomes continuous, the mechanical seal should be repaired or replaced.

3. FLANGE

The discharge flange should be inspected for surface corrosion.

Minor corrosion should be cleaned and monitored.

Severe corrosion requires engineering evaluation and corrective maintenance.

4. ALIGNMENT

Pump shaft alignment should be checked during scheduled maintenance.

Alignment deviation greater than 1.0 mm requires corrective action.

5. FOLLOW-UP INSPECTION

After completing maintenance, a follow-up inspection should be performed.

The equipment should only be returned to normal service after maintenance findings have been reviewed.

6. SAFETY

Maintenance personnel must follow applicable plant safety procedures and use appropriate personal protective equipment.
